About Me

Aspiring game developer majoring in Game and Simulation Programming at the Pennsylvania College of Technology, currently in my junior year with a 4.0 GPA. Proficient in C#, C++, and Python, with experience developing games in Unity, Unreal Engine 5, and GameMaker Studio. Additionally skilled in web development, demonstrated through the creation of this portfolio website.

My goal is to become a professional game developer creating experiences that players love. I thrive at the intersection of creativity and technical skill, and I am eager to contribute to innovative, engaging games.

Notable projects include Escapism (Unity, C#), M.O.R.Z. Purge (Unity, C#), "Reality" (Unity, C#), and Project M.O.R.Z. (GameMaker, GML). Most are available to demo directly on this website.

  • Escapism is a narrative puzzle game where players complete randomized puzzles scattered across the map to escape each level.
  • M.O.R.Z. Purge is a first-person action shooter in which the player must kill hostile enemies as they navigate through an alien cave system.
  • "Reality" is a realistic puzzle game where the player must collect objects on the map the assist their progression towards the exit.
  • Project M.O.R.Z. is a top-down action shooter in which the player pilots a tank on a hostile alien planet. They must destroy alien bases, collect money, and upgrade their vehicle to face increasingly difficult challenges.
