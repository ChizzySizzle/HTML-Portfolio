M.O.R.Z. Purge

M.O.R.Z. Purge is an action-packed sci-fi first-person shooter.
The Human Planetary Services Corporation (H.P.S.) was in the process of collecting resources through mining operations on a newly found planet, Zorapterra, when they breached into an unknown alien-inhabited section of cave. These bug-like aliens ravaged the humans and continue to prevent any further resource collection on the planet. H.P.S. has given you, the player, the orders to completely clear out the alien-infested cave system on planet Zorapterra and reclaim the resources that are rightfully ours.
